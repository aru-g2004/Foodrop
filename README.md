# Foodrop

Welcome to the repo for Foodrop, an app developed for the Congressional App Challenge 2022, a national mobile application development competition! Foodrop is designed to bring communities together to volunteer, reduce food waste, and feed neighbors in need. Our team won the Congressional App Challenge from New Jersey and had the honor of presenting the application at the Capitol Hill building in April to members of Congress, the D.C. community, and our Congresswoman Bonnie Watson Coleman.

Check out the [official Congressional App Challenge website](https://www.congressionalappchallenge.us/2022-winners/#NewJersey) to learn more!
